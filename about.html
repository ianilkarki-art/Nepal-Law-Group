Our Vision

To evolve into a nationally esteemed institutional platform that advances the rule of law, legal scholarship, and professional integrity – while ensuring that every member both contributes to and benefits from a culture of excellence.

Our Mission

To create a self‑sustaining legal community wherein practising advocates, legal researchers, academics, and publishers collaborate synergistically. The Group shall distribute no profit; its entire revenue (derived exclusively from membership fees) will be reinvested into legal research, academic programmes, publications, and institutional development.

Our Foundation

The Group’s activities will be strictly service‑oriented, non‑profit, educational, academic, social, charitable, and of public utility. This foundational character is embedded in the Group’s proposed Memorandum of Association, which prohibits the distribution of any surplus to members and mandates that all revenue be applied solely toward the achievement of its stated objects.

    Our Efforts

    We endeavour to elevate legal excellence across Nepal through structured collaboration, interdisciplinary networking, and the continuous sharing of legal knowledge. This includes the establishment of legal aid clinics, the organisation of annual colloquia on emerging areas of law, the development of open‑access digital repositories of Nepalese jurisprudence, and the cultivation of partnerships with academic institutions both within Nepal and abroad.
